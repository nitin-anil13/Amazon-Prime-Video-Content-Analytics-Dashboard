# 🎬 Amazon Prime Video Content Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The Amazon Prime Video Content Analytics Dashboard is a Business Intelligence project designed to analyze Amazon Prime Video's content library and uncover insights related to content growth, genre distribution, audience engagement, ratings, and geographic footprint.

Using Python for data analysis and Power BI for visualization, this project transforms raw streaming data into actionable business insights that can support content strategy, regional expansion, and audience retention decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

With thousands of movies and TV shows available on Amazon Prime Video, understanding content trends and viewer preferences is critical for maintaining a competitive advantage in the streaming industry.

This project aims to answer key business questions:

* How has Amazon Prime's content library evolved over time?
* Which genres dominate the platform?
* Which countries contribute the most content?
* How do IMDb and TMDB ratings compare across titles?
* What opportunities exist for content diversification and regional growth?

---

## 📊 Dataset Information

The dataset contains:

### Titles Dataset

* 9,000+ unique movies and TV shows
* 15 attributes including:

  * Title
  * Show Type
  * Release Year
  * Genres
  * Runtime
  * IMDb Score
  * IMDb Votes
  * TMDB Popularity
  * TMDB Score
  * Production Countries

### Credits Dataset

* 124,000+ actor and director records
* Includes:

  * Actor/Director Name
  * Character Name
  * Role Type
  * Title ID

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Content Growth

* Content releases surged significantly during the 2010s, making it the most dominant decade on the platform.
* Amazon Prime's library is heavily focused on modern content.

### Content Mix

* Movies account for over 93% of the platform's catalog.
* TV Shows represent a relatively small portion of available content.

### Genre Analysis

* Drama, Action, and Comedy are the most represented genres.
* Several niche genres remain underrepresented, creating opportunities for diversification.

### Geographic Footprint

* Content originates from 171 countries.
* The United States contributes the largest share of titles, followed by India and the United Kingdom.

### Ratings & Engagement

* Average IMDb Score: 6.03
* Average TMDB Score: 6.07
* Most titles receive moderate ratings, indicating opportunities for higher-quality content investments.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Expand investment in regional and local-language content.
* Strengthen original TV show production to improve audience retention.
* Promote high-rated but less-discovered titles to increase viewer engagement.
* Diversify content categories by investing in underrepresented genres.
* Prioritize quality-focused content strategies to improve platform reputation.
